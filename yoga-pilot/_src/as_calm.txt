Calm is the #1 app for sleep, meditation, and relaxation. Find anxiety relief, stress relief, and better sleep through breathing exercises, sleep sounds, deep sleep music, and mindfulness programs for insomnia, beginner meditation, and beyond. Downloaded by 180 million people worldwide.
Fall asleep faster with Sleep Stories, bedtime stories for adults narrated by Matthew McConaughey, Stephen Fry, P\!nk, Bob Ross, and more. Only on Calm.
Manage stress, relieve anxiety, improve your sleep cycle, and refocus your attention.
Apple App of the Year. Apple Editors' Choice. Happiest App in the World (Center for Humane Technology). 3M+ five-star reviews. Recommended by top psychologists, therapists, and mental health experts.

* Travel through the village of Provence with master storyteller Stephen Fry in Blue Gold

MEDITATION & MINDFULNESS

* 7- and 21-day mindfulness programs to build a lasting practice
ANXIETY RELIEF & RELAXATION
* Breathing exercises and breathwork to ease anxiety, stress, and insomnia
* Bilateral stimulation music and neuroacoustic sounds for nervous system regulation and somatic relaxation
* Guided breathwork for stress relief, anxiety relief, and deep calm

* Mindful movement and stretching with Daily Move
SLEEP SOUNDS, MUSIC & SOUNDSCAPES

I really love this app.  I tried a few other meditation apps before trying Calm, but I didn't like them. I love the Calm meditations.   They are partially guided and offer wonderful advice on so many topics, such as self esteem, not responding to thoughts and emotions, compassion, procrastination, kindness, and so many others. Tamara's voice is so soothing and inviting, I could listen to her every day for the rest of my life, and I hope she never stops doing the Calm meditations.  She is so great.   I highly recommend the Calm app.

Like therapy in my pocket!
12/28/2020
miss flack <3
I have suffered from severe panic disorder, GAD, depression, and OCD since age 12 (I am 30 now). I have been in therapy since then, but I am only able to afford it on a biweekly basis, when in reality I need support weekly if not more. My therapist recommended this app to me while in the midst of a relapse last year, and I downloaded it figuring if I didn’t like it I could just cancel after the free trial. I didn’t expect much.This app is a literal GODSEND for people struggling with mental illness. The 7 Days of Calming Anxiety and 21 Days of Calm have been lifesaving programs for me. I repeat them as needed and they are a great supplement to my regular therapy sessions! I feel immediately so relaxed after practicing them, even if I was in the midst of a 5 alarm panic attack right before that. At night and on weekends when I don’t have access to a mental health professional, or in between sessions, this is a lifesaver. It’s like having therapy in your pocket. Not only is Tamara’s voice incredibly soothing, but she takes the time to actually explain anxiety and how it works, which makes me feel less alone or less silly and reassured me when I’m feeling crazy bodily symptoms. For people saying that it’s expensive: I understand 80 dollars a year is not something all of us have, especially in times like these, but when you really think about the cost of therapy per year, this is a much more affordable and helpful option in a pinch. Best money I’ve ever spent, especially during uncertain times like these.
